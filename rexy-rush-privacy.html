Rexy Rush: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Rexy Rush ("the game") is made by Dragonforge Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress, stars, settings and unlocked items are saved only on your own device, and are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and share:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location, based on your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics and crash logs

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings under Google → Ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads and the Starter Pack) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Data sent by the advertising and billing services is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
