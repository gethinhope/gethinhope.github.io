Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Dragonforge Studios ("we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile games and apps (the "Apps"). It applies to:

  • Purrfect Match – Cat Match 3
  • Doggo Dash
  • Guardians of the Realm

By using our Apps you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Apps.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location through our Apps.

Game progress (such as levels completed, stars, in-game coins and settings) is stored on your device. If you uninstall the App, this data may be lost.

Our Apps use the third-party services listed below, which may automatically collect some information from your device.

2. Third-party services

3. How the information is used

  • To show advertisements, which keep our games free to play
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To find and fix crashes and improve performance
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising and consent

Where required by law (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area), the App will ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the App's privacy settings, where available.

You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).

5. Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our Apps, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Data retention

We do not keep any personal information on our own servers. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Third-party providers keep data according to their own retention policies linked above.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (including under UK GDPR and EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, and to object to or restrict how it is used.

Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, you can remove all game data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings. For data held by our advertising and analytics partners, you can reset your Advertising ID as described above, or contact us and we will help you with your request.

To make a request, email dragonforgestudios90@gmail.com with the subject "Data request" and the name of the App. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we only work with established providers such as Google and Unity.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
