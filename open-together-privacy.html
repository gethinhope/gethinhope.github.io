Open Together: Logic Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Open Together: Logic Puzzle ("the game") is made by Dragonforge Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no adverts and no analytics. Your progress, stars, awards and settings are saved only on your own device, and are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

The game offers one optional purchase that unlocks the full game. It is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The game contains no adverts and does not use any advertising service.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Data sent by the billing service is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
